* The two findings that matter to this repository

** The raw inputs of the 2021 and 2023 editions do not exist in the folder

=data-raw/= holds only =IOTs_2025_RData/=, and =archive/legacy_data/= holds no
ICIO matrices of either edition.
=NIOTs_2021.rdata= and =NIOTs_2023.rdata= therefore cannot be regenerated there,
and they carry the results the reporting stage currently rests on.
That is why =data-processed/= is deposited, and the reason is irreproducibility
rather than convenience.

** The boundary objects are not the same file

=data-processed/NIOTs_2023.rdata= binds one object named =NIOTs=: a list of *74*
countries, each with 14 slots (=CIn=, =Fn=, =CIm=, =Fm=, =CI=, =Ft=, =Recurso=,
=lista_An=, =lista_Am=, =lista_A=, =lista_Z=, =Ghosh=, =lista_BL=, =lista_FL=),
each slot a list of 26 years of matrices.

=etc/Modelo/NIOTs_resultados.rdata= holds *75* countries.

This is evidence for tickets #26 and #27, and it also explains the
order-of-magnitude puzzle stated in the body of #37: the two copies differing by
a factor of ten are two *editions* of the OECD National IOTs, 8.2 MB for 2023
against 126.3 MB for 2021, and not WIOD-43 against OECD-75.
The correspondence with the 8.7 MB and 88 MB copies in this repository is
suggestive and was not established.

* Outstanding, and able to change what is deposited

The licence statement on the OECD National Input-Output Tables page was not
verified: the page returns HTTP 403 to this environment.
If it states terms more restrictive than CC BY 4.0, =data-raw/= leaves the
deposit and the =Makefile= instructs a download from the publisher instead.
