#+title: ADR-0011: The XR gap diagnosis reads currency inputs directly
#+date: 2026-07-21
#+filetags: :IODefl:diagnosis:adr:

* Status

Accepted, 2026-07-21.

* Context

The exchange-rate GDP growth-gap diagnosis needs a per-country nominal exchange
rate $e_t$ and GDP deflator $P_t$.
The diagnosis plan sourced =code/tmp.R= and called =get_Erate()= and
=get_deflator()= to obtain them.
Those helpers do not run: =tmp.R= fails to parse (a stray bracket at
=code/tmp.R:391=), and even once parsed the readers carry defects
(=get_deflator()= references an undefined =fdefl= and a bare global
=OCDE_country=; =get_common()= reads a mistyped, unprefixed =agregador.xslx=).
ADR-0010 already excludes these Stage-A helpers from v1 and records their
global-reference defect as out of scope.

* Decision

The diagnosis reads the cleaned currency inputs directly:
=inputs/exchange_rate_OCDE.xlsx= and =inputs/deflator_GDP_OCDE.xlsx=, filling
countries absent from the OECD source from the World Bank variants, inside pure
functions in =code/diagnose_xr_gap.R=.
It does not source =tmp.R= and does not depend on the excluded Stage-A helpers.

* Considered Options

Fixing =tmp.R= was rejected: it contradicts ADR-0010 and pulls work-in-progress
Stage-A code into the diagnosis.
Using the World Bank series alone for both $e$ and $P$ was rejected: it discards
the OECD primary source and weakens Hypothesis H2, which needs the
OECD-versus-World-Bank-fallback split.

* Consequences

The diagnosis is self-contained and testable against the supplied
=NIOTs_resultados.rdata= without a runnable Stage A.
The reader records each country's provenance so H2 can identify the
World-Bank-fallback subset.
The OECD exchange-rate input expresses the rate as local-currency units per US
dollar, the inverse of the direction stated in the diagnosis spec; the reader
inverts it to US dollars per local-currency unit so the central prediction's
unit-one slope holds as written.
If Stage A becomes runnable, the stronger fixed-rate reconstruction of Rung 3
supersedes the algebraic counterfactual used in v1.
