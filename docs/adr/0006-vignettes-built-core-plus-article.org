#+title: ADR-0006: Built core vignette plus non-built data article
#+date: 2026-07-08
#+filetags: :IODefl:growthdecomp:adr:

* Status

Accepted, 2026-07-08.

* Context

CRAN builds vignettes at check time and cannot reach the network or use keys.
The package must both demonstrate the decomposition and instruct users on fetching public data.

* Decision

Vignette one, "Decomposing growth", runs fully on the bundled =niot_wiod= object and builds on CRAN.
Vignette two, "Fetching public data", is a non-built pkgdown article with =eval = FALSE= or precomputed output, showing World Bank, FRED, and UN retrieval and =.Renviron= setup.

* Consequences

The core narrative executes and cannot drift from the code.
The data-fetching narrative is discoverable without breaking the CRAN build.
The article is not verified at check time and must be reviewed manually when the fetchers change.
