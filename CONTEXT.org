#+title: CONTEXT: Deflating WIOD Tables / Growth Decomposition
#+filetags: :IODefl:context:

This repository decomposes real GDP growth into domestic and import-content
contributions across countries, and diagnoses why the growth computed from the
deflated input-output tables diverges from official growth.
This file is the glossary of terms specific to that domain.

* Language

** IO growth ($g^{IO}$)
Real GDP growth computed from the deflated national input-output tables, built
on the expenditure side in =prepare_data()=.
_Avoid_: model growth, computed growth.

** Official growth ($g^{off}$)
Benchmark real GDP growth from an external source, primarily the World Bank
constant-US-dollar series =NY.GDP.MKTP.KD=, which converts at a fixed base-year
rate.
_Avoid_: true growth, actual growth.

** Gap
The difference $g^{IO} - g^{off}$ for a country-year, the object the diagnosis
explains.

** Deflation state
Whether the supplied input-output series is real (the $e/P$ conversion applied),
nominal in local currency (nothing applied), or nominal in dollars (multiplied
by $e$ only).
It is unknown for a supplied object and resolved empirically, not assumed.

** WB-fallback country
A country whose exchange-rate or deflator series is taken from the World Bank
because it is absent from the OECD source.
The subset is the discriminating variable for Hypothesis H2.
_Avoid_: missing country, imputed country.

** Exchange-rate unit convention
The rate $e$ is expressed as US dollars per unit of local currency, so a
depreciation lowers $e$.
The OECD input file stores the inverse (local-currency units per US dollar, US
value one) and is inverted on read.
_Avoid_: FX, parity.
